I am currently an associate professor at [IRMA](https://irma.math.unistra.fr/) (Strasbourg university). 

## Research interests

My work focuses on building robust decision-support tools for complex systems. This research is divided into two main areas:

*   **System Understanding & Biostatistics:** Analyzing **(multi)-omic data** (e.g., RNA regulation datasets) using advanced machine learning and statistical methods to understand genomic mechanisms.
*   **Decision Strategies & Stochastic Control:** Developing decision-making strategies using **stochastic control theory** on **(Bayesian) Markov processes**, solved via **(deep) reinforcement learning** algorithms.

In both areas, I am strongly interested in **healthcare applications**.

Please feel free to reach out if my research interests you!
